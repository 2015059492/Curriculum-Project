Immune System Learning Stations

Learners move through five makerspace-inspired stations. Each station supports exploration, interaction, creation, application, and reflection.

Station 1: Explore Zone

Learners watch short videos and explore visual explanations to build foundational understanding of immunity and immune responses.

Open Immunity Presentation Watch HIV Immunity Watch Double Immunity

Station 2: Game & Simulation Zone

Learners use games and simulations to visualize how the immune system detects and responds to pathogens.

Open Immune Patrol Open Immune Quest Open BioInteractive Open Labster Resource

Station 3: Build Zone

Learners construct a physical or digital model showing interactions between pathogens, antibodies, white blood cells, and immune responses.

Start Build Activity

Station 4: Apply Zone

Learners apply their understanding to real-world scenarios involving infections, vaccines, antibiotics, and immune system responses.

Start Application Task

Station 5: AI & Reflection Zone

Learners use AI-supported tools and reflection prompts to explain immune processes, compare ideas, and refine their understanding.
