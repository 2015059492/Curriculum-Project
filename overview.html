Curriculum Overview

This curriculum project presents a makerspace-inspired, technology-enhanced learning environment designed to support conceptual understanding of immunity in Grade 11 Life Sciences. The learning experience integrates simulations, games, inquiry activities, model construction, and reflective learning through structured learning stations.

The module encourages active participation, inquiry-based learning, collaboration, and the use of digital tools to support deeper engagement with abstract biological processes.
